Advanced

Text is sent in Windows-1252. If accents come out wrong, look for «WPC1252» or «1252» on the self-test page of the printer (usually printed by holding FEED while turning it on) and write its number here. It is almost always 16.

Help
  • Bluetooth Classic printers: pair them in the operating system first. Windows: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device (the PIN is often 0000 or 1234); macOS: System Settings > Bluetooth. The printer then becomes a serial port (on Windows a COM port, «Standard Serial over Bluetooth link») and appears in the list of «Choose port» (Chrome and Edge 117 or later also list paired Bluetooth printers directly).
  • USB printers with a serial chip (CH340, PL2303, FTDI, CP210x; on Windows «USB Serial Device» or a COM port in Device Manager): if nothing prints, or random characters come out, set the speed in the «Printer» card. On Windows the chip may need the driver of its maker.
  • Printers installed in the system and network printers cannot be reached from a browser: a USB printer that Windows lists in «Printers & scanners» with its own driver (not as a COM port) and the Ethernet or Wi-Fi printers (port 9100) are not serial ports, and Web Serial sees only those. What they would need: docs/desktop-companion.md.
  • The port does not open. Only one program at a time can use a serial port: close the printer utility, cash register programs and the other tabs of this page, then press «Reconnect». After unplugging the printer, plug it in again and press «Reconnect» (or «Choose port»). «Disconnect» frees the port for other programs.
  • Chosen once. Chrome remembers the ports allowed for this site: on the next visit the page reopens the port by itself, without taps. Some USB adapters without a serial number must be chosen again after being unplugged.
  • Random characters or wrong accents. Random characters: wrong speed (USB-serial printers), choose another one in the «Printer» card. Wrong accents: set the code page in «Advanced».
  • Firefox, Safari, iPhone, iPad and Android do not support Web Serial. On Android use the Bluetooth LE page (Bluetooth LE printers) or the Print Bridge app (Bluetooth Classic printers).
